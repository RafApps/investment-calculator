Plan settings

Most Puerto Rico plans cap your contributions at $15,000 a year. "PR dual" means a dual-qualified plan with a higher limit, which drops to $22,500 if you also contribute to an IRA. Ask HR which one yours is. From age 50 a catch-up is added on top.
